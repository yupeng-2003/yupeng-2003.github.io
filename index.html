01About

Curious about intelligence in the loop.

I joined the PhD program in Artificial Intelligence at Xi'an Jiaotong University in September 2024. I am advised by Prof. Hongbin Sun and Prof. Xuchong Zhang at the Institute of Artificial Intelligence and Robotics.

Before that, I received my B.S. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from Xi'an Jiaotong University. My mathematical training continues to shape how I think about learning systems: from first principles, with attention to structure and failure.

Advisors Hongbin Sun · Xuchong Zhang
